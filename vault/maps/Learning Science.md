Map of Content for how learning actually works — evidence-based techniques for
durable understanding and memory. The recurring theme is *desirable
difficulty*: the methods that feel harder during study tend to produce the
strongest long-term retention.

## Notes
- [[Active Recall]] — retrieving information from memory instead of rereading it.
- [[Spaced Repetition]] — reviewing across increasing intervals instead of cramming.

## How they connect
Active recall and spaced repetition are two halves of the same strategy:
*spacing* decides **when** to review, *recall* decides **how**. Combine them —
spaced reviews done as retrieval practice — for the biggest payoff.
