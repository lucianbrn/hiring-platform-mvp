title: Active Recall

Learning by *retrieving* information from memory instead of reviewing it.
Rather than rereading my notes, I close them and try to reconstruct the idea —
self-testing, answering questions, or explaining it aloud. The effort of pulling
something out of memory is what strengthens it, which is why practice tests beat
rereading even though rereading feels easier. The discomfort is the point: a
little struggle during recall is a "desirable difficulty" that makes the memory
durable.

## Related
- [[Spaced Repetition]] — spacing decides *when* I review; active recall decides

  attempt, not a passive reread.
